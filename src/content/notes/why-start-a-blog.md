Why would an aging programmer with questionable talent start a blog in the age
of AI, when everyone says programming is dead?

Well, precisely because of that.

I see a lot of people giving up in the face of what they call the new industrial
revolution.

I don't agree with the way many people see things when it comes to whether
being a programmer is still worthwhile.

And while a blog by some programmer who came out of nowhere will barely be read
by anyone, I want to raise this flag from here, the last human stronghold.

Yes, all right, that was a little dramatic, but I like the idea of writing
again. And I'm writing without using artificial intelligence to replace me in
this work.

I'm not saying I don't use it. Of course I do. Just not for writing, not for
work that's distinctly human.

For example, tonight I used a free Chinese model to build myself a blog in
Astro. It only took me a few minutes.

But writing? Well, that's another matter.

And what could I write about?

How about this verbose, useless “Hello, world”?

A “Hello, world” that closes the loop I began with my first programming
language, a modern, wordy version of something so simple, perfectly suited to
the times we live in.

So there you have it: hidden between these lines, you can see my manifesto.
Maybe it's proof that even among the chaff, the needle can still be found?

Who knows? The odds say this post is more likely to become another abandoned
relic, one of countless others in the vast ocean of the Internet. But I assure
you, my friend, nothing is decided until you dare to write it.

Well, this is my message, and I hope I can write a second one.

See you!
