A small CLI that bundles the developer utilities I reach for daily, so I can
format, check, and scaffold without switching context.

## Why it exists

Most "developer tool" suites are bloated or demand config I don't need. This
one stays intentionally small: a handful of commands, sensible defaults, and
no config required to get value.

## Status

Early and experimental. It solves my own workflow first; if it helps yours,
the repo is open.
